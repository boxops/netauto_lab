ℹ️ Welcome to Clano

Network incidents get investigated and fixed automatically — without you needing to dig through logs or run commands manually. When something goes wrong, Clano works through five steps and stops for your approval before touching anything:

1 · Investigate what broke → 2 · Suggest a fix → 3 · Check the fix is safe → 4 · Wait for your approval ← you are here → 5 · Apply the fix & confirm it worked

Select an incident on the left to see its full timeline. Use ⚙️ Config to control how much the AI can do on its own. Use 💬 Assist to ask questions about the network in plain English.
