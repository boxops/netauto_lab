🤖 Autonomy Policies
What is an autonomy policy?
Each policy governs one alert type end-to-end across two activation points: (1) Fast path — if you define conditions, the pipeline checks them before calling the AI; if every condition passes, the RCA and fix are generated from your templates in seconds with zero LLM calls. (2) Approval gate — the autonomy level (L0–L5) controls what happens after the AI (or after the fast path): L2 waits for your click, L4 auto-approves when confidence and risk thresholds are met. Leave the fast-path section empty and the policy acts as a gate-only rule. The Learning Engine automatically promotes or demotes the level based on whether past fixes actually resolved the alert.

🎯 Standing Intents
What are standing intents?
Intents are persistent declarations about how the network should behave. Unlike policies (which control how much autonomy the AI uses), intents control when the AI acts and what it does with specific alert types.
