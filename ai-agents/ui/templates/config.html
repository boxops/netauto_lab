🤖 Autonomy Policies
What are autonomy policies?
Policies tell the AI how much independence it has for each type of network fix. A BGP peer reset on a lab leaf might be set to L4 (auto-approve) after a few successful runs, while the same fix on a production spine stays at L2 (human must click Approve). The Learning Engine automatically promotes or demotes a policy based on whether past fixes actually resolved the alert — so the system earns autonomy incrementally rather than being granted it upfront.

🎯 Standing Intents
What are standing intents?
Intents are persistent declarations about how the network should behave. Unlike policies (which control how much autonomy the AI uses), intents control when the AI acts and what it does with specific alert types.
